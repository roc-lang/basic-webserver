Each process constructs one persistent production SseBody around one reusable
4,096-byte Datastar-shaped item and one 4,096-byte frame slot. It advances 2,048
warmup events before taking allocator counters. Corpus construction, stream
construction, and normal FINISH are outside the measured window. Returned
frames are synchronously consumed and dropped. This isolates the production
body, encoder, source polling, pool reservation, ServerData wrapping, Body
polling, and frame release; it does not include Hyper or socket allocations.
